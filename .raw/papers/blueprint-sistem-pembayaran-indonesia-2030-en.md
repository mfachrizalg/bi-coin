CHAPTER V
      CONCLUDING
      REMARKS
      The continuity of national digital transformation needs to be persistently supported by a robust
      payment system ecosystem that can meet public needs. The IPSB 2030 aims to continue the
      noble goals of IPSB 2025, creating an efficient and inclusive national digital financial ecosystem,
      and supporting the integration of the national digital economy to achieve Golden Indonesia 2045.
      All initiatives under IPSB 2030 will be carried out in collaboration with Ministries and Agencies, and
      relevant private sectors.
      The rapid progress of the national DEF will continue to be strengthened to support the future
      national economic recovery. The national digital transformation, which over the past five years has
      engendered profound and transformative changes in Indonesian society, is expected to continue
      and even expand amid increasingly complex and uncertain global dynamics. Bank Indonesia’s future
      payment system policies will continue to be directed at strengthening payment system stability and
      supporting further acceleration of the national DEF.
      Policy synergy and collaborative efforts from the private sectors, government, and regulators
      are imperative to oversee the continuity of national digitization. The implementation of the five
      IPSB 2030 initiatives would be unattainable for Bank Indonesia without close synergy, coordination,
      and collaboration with various stakeholders. Therefore, efforts to strengthen synergy, coordination,
      and collaboration with the private sectors, government, and other relevant authorities must be
      continually pursued and further augmented through transparent and enduring communication
      channels.
      The future is here and now. Overall, IPSB 2030 will facilitate the establishment of a more
      prosperous and equitable future for the forthcoming generations of Indonesia (Figure 16). A smooth
      payment system, along with well-functioning monetary and financial system stability, will serve as
      the foundation for sustainable economic growth. IPSB 2030 represents Bank Indonesia’s tangible
      contribution to cultivating a robust digital economic and financial ecosystem while ensuring the
      fulfilment of the duties and authorities as the central bank. Furthermore, IPSB 2030 will weave the
      grand efforts of Indonesia’s structural economic reforms towards digital transformation, integrating
      the participation of all economic actors, both large and small, at the central and regional levels, within
