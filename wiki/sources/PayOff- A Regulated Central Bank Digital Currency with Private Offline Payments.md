# PayOff: A Regulated Central Bank Digital Currency with Private Offline Payments

## Evidence-backed summary

- Offline payments are expected to enable cash-like privacy, local payment settlement, and the enforcement of holding limits. ([[.raw/papers/payoff-a-regulated-central-bank-digital-currency-with-private-offline-payments#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The main contribution of this paper is PayOff, a novel CBDC design motivated by the digital euro regulation, which focuses on offline payments. ([[.raw/papers/payoff-a-regulated-central-bank-digital-currency-with-private-offline-payments#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- We analyze the security implications of local payment settlement and identify new security objectives. ([[.raw/papers/payoff-a-regulated-central-bank-digital-currency-with-private-offline-payments#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- PayOff protects user privacy, supports complex regulations such as holding limits, and implements safeguards to increase robustness against secure element failure. ([[.raw/papers/payoff-a-regulated-central-bank-digital-currency-with-private-offline-payments#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Our analysis shows that PayOff provides strong privacy and identifies residual leakages that may arise in real-world deployments. ([[.raw/papers/payoff-a-regulated-central-bank-digital-currency-with-private-offline-payments#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- I NTRODUCTION The idea of a central bank digital currency (CBDC) has gained increasing attention. ([[.raw/papers/payoff-a-regulated-central-bank-digital-currency-with-private-offline-payments#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Notably, the European Central Bank (ECB) has communicated its plans to introduce a digital euro [24] as retail CBDC, i.e., accessible to the general public, and the European Commission has recently released a proposal [22] on its regulation. ([[.raw/papers/payoff-a-regulated-central-bank-digital-currency-with-private-offline-payments#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- We evaluate the performance of PayOff through a prototype implementation. (PDF p. 2).
- The account model has certain advantages. (PDF p. 5).

## Limitations and cautions

- While other central banks have expressed similar desired functionality, achieving such offline payments poses a novel technical challenge. (PDF p. 1).
- However, the main limitation of PayOff is that offline payment messages and storage requirements grow in the number of payments that the sender makes or receives without going online in between. (PDF p. 1).
