## Supplied study type

Official central-bank white paper documenting the design, scope, and PoC methodology of Indonesia's wholesale CBDC initiative (Digital Rupiah).

## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Describes the wholesale CBDC architecture using permissioned blockchain with Proof-of-Authority (PoA) consensus, with Bank Indonesia as a transaction validator integrated with the BI-RTGS system. Documents the Immediate State PoC completed in August 2024, which tested R3 Corda and Kaleido Hyperledger Besu across 55 scenarios on AWS infrastructure, validating RTGS-triggered and platform-triggered modes.
