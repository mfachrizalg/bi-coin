## Evidence-backed summary

- The success of CBDC programmes will be closely tied to the public interest in adopting CBDC. ([[.raw/papers/cbdc-adoption-aligning-model-technostress-inhibitors-and-perceived-value-among-indonesian-generation-z#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Given the emerging influence of those born into what is commonly known as Generation Z, this research involved 329 Indonesians identifying as Generation Z to determine factors affecting the adoption of Indonesian CBDC. ([[.raw/papers/cbdc-adoption-aligning-model-technostress-inhibitors-and-perceived-value-among-indonesian-generation-z#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The results show that the main determinants driving Generation Z’s interest in adopting CBDC are perceived value (epistemic, monetary, and convenience value) and general trust. ([[.raw/papers/cbdc-adoption-aligning-model-technostress-inhibitors-and-perceived-value-among-indonesian-generation-z#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- General trust in this study also shows a partial moderating effect in the relationship between perceived value and intention to use CBDC and a full moderation effect on the relationship between technostress inhibitors and intention to use CBDC. ([[.raw/papers/cbdc-adoption-aligning-model-technostress-inhibitors-and-perceived-value-among-indonesian-generation-z#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The findings of this study provide advice to Bank Indonesia on how to increase the usefulness of CBDC related to monetary value and the value derived from the ease of use of CBDC to maintain public trust and increase public interest. ([[.raw/papers/cbdc-adoption-aligning-model-technostress-inhibitors-and-perceived-value-among-indonesian-generation-z#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The development of the digital economy has encouraged the birth of digital money, known as Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC). ([[.raw/papers/cbdc-adoption-aligning-model-technostress-inhibitors-and-perceived-value-among-indonesian-generation-z#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Central banks worldwide are developing CBDC to replace or complement fiat currency. ([[.raw/papers/cbdc-adoption-aligning-model-technostress-inhibitors-and-perceived-value-among-indonesian-generation-z#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- CBDC Adoption: Aligning Model Technostress Inhibitors and Perceived Value Among Indonesian Generation Z 51 to introduce CBDC as an official currency to overcome monetary volatility regarding the use of crypto assets and aim to increase financial inclusiveness in Indonesia. (PDF p. 3).
- Bank Indonesia released a conceptual CBDC model in a White Paper in 2022. (PDF p. 3).

## Limitations and cautions

- Academics and governments have widely explored research on CBDC acceptance.12 However, there has been little research regarding interest in the adoption of CBDC broken out by generation. (PDF p. 4).
- This research considers privacy risk, perceived value, technostress inhibitors, and general trust to determine the main factors influencing Generation Z’s interest in CBDC. (PDF p. 4).
