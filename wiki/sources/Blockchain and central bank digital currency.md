## Evidence-backed summary

- However, it is still controversial on adoption of blockchain in CBDC design. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-and-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- In the paper, we analyze both functional and non-functional requirements of CBDC design, and make a literature review on blockchain based CBDC schemes. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-and-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Analysis findings show that permissioned blockchain is more suitable for CBDC than permissionless blockchain. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-and-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Besides, there are some challenges in blockchain based CBDC, such as performance, scalability, and cross-chain interoperability. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-and-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Our analysis is timely and can provide guidelines for blockchain based CBDC design. c 2021 The Authors. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-and-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Blockchain is the backbone technology of Bitcoin and breakthroughs on blockchain have made a great influence on payment methods, e-commerce and cross-border transfer. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-and-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Blockchain technology is also widely used in cryptocurrencies and more than 5000 blockchain based cryptocurrencies have emerged in the past 10 years [2]. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-and-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- As the key technology of most cryptocurrencies, blockchain is also explored in CBDC researches and prototype experiments. (PDF p. 1).
- In [5], World Economic Forum makes a survey on how central banks use blockchain to solve financial and monetary challenges, experimentation and implementation of CBDC are also involved. (PDF p. 2).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, it is still controversial on adoption of blockchain in CBDC design. (PDF p. 1).
- However, it is still controversial on adoption of blockchain in CBDC design, because there are still many challenges of blockchain technology to overcome, such as security, throughout and scalability issues. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icte.2021.09.014 c 2021 The Authors. (PDF p. 1).
