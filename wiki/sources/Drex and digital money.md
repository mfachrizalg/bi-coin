## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Characterizes Brazil's DREX as a 'disrupted experiment' embodying a fundamental conflict between technological ambition and institutional reality. Documents the inability to resolve the privacy trilemma on Hyperledger Besu, leading to BCB's major architectural change after two years of investment with 16 consortia.
