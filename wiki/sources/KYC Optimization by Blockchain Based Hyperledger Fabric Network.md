## Evidence-backed summary

- The adoption of disruptive technology is a necessary condition for the coming future of financial institutions. ([[.raw/papers/kyc-optimization-by-blockchain-based-hyperledger-fabric-network#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This study proposed a Hyperledger Fabric network for KYC optimization. ([[.raw/papers/kyc-optimization-by-blockchain-based-hyperledger-fabric-network#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The Performance of the proposed system was tested by using the Hyperledger Composer. ([[.raw/papers/kyc-optimization-by-blockchain-based-hyperledger-fabric-network#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The experiment results confirm that due to the strong and identity features of Hyperledger Fabric, the proposed system can speed up the KYC clearing transfers, challenge the inefficiencies that arise from duplicated conduct of similar tasks, secure data sharing, cost-effective, and ultimately bring transparency in the traditional KYC system. ([[.raw/papers/kyc-optimization-by-blockchain-based-hyperledger-fabric-network#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Blockchain technology would enable banks to get a shared KYC system. ([[.raw/papers/kyc-optimization-by-blockchain-based-hyperledger-fabric-network#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- In the future, if financial institution needs to check customer KYC records, they can get them from blockchain based KYC data sharing system. ([[.raw/papers/kyc-optimization-by-blockchain-based-hyperledger-fabric-network#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- In public blockchain such as Ethereum [6], anybody can set up a node and join the system. ([[.raw/papers/kyc-optimization-by-blockchain-based-hyperledger-fabric-network#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- The experiment results confirm that due to the strong and identity features of Hyperledger Fabric, the proposed system can speed up the KYC clearing transfers, challenge the inefficiencies that arise from duplicated conduct of similar tasks, secure data sharing, cost-effective, and ultimately bring transparency in the traditional KYC system. (PDF p. 1).
- As per the survey, budgetary establishments with $10 billion or more have seen their regular revenue on KYC process upsurge to 150 million US dollar this year from 142 million US dollars in 2016. (PDF p. 1).

## Limitations and cautions
