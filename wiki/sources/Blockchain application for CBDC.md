## Supplied study type

Technical analysis of consensus mechanisms suitable for CBDC applications, focused on protocol-level characteristics rather than national-scale deployments.

## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Finds that permissioned BFT-family protocols are best suited for CBDC applications due to deterministic finality and energy efficiency compared to Proof-of-Work alternatives. Frames consensus selection at a theoretical-protocol level rather than empirical national-scale performance.
