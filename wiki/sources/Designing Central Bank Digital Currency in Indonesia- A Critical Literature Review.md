# Designing Central Bank Digital Currency in Indonesia: A Critical Literature Review

## Evidence-backed summary

- This paper aims to assess whether the proposed design of r-Digital Rupiah is suitable for Indonesia by conducting a comparative critical literature review of the Project Garuda: Navigating the Architecture of Digital Rupiah (2022) white paper by Bank Indonesia. ([[.raw/papers/designing-central-bank-digital-currency-in-indonesia-a-critical-literature-review#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- In doing so, it will examine, analyze, and synthesize the existing literature on CBDCs to extract key information pertaining to the design frameworks of Indonesia's CBDC. ([[.raw/papers/designing-central-bank-digital-currency-in-indonesia-a-critical-literature-review#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- A comparative analysis is also conducted to explore similarities, differences, and potential implications by comparing the designs of the retail CBDC of Indonesia with those implemented in other countries. ([[.raw/papers/designing-central-bank-digital-currency-in-indonesia-a-critical-literature-review#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- The findings from this study contributes to the understanding of the CBDC's design, identify potential strengths and weaknesses, and provide recommendations for optimizing its design to ensure its effectiveness, compliance, and compatibility with international standards and practices. ([[.raw/papers/designing-central-bank-digital-currency-in-indonesia-a-critical-literature-review#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- This critique argues that further research and analysis are necessary to fully understand the potential implications of Digital Rupiah. ([[.raw/papers/designing-central-bank-digital-currency-in-indonesia-a-critical-literature-review#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- Dalam melakukannya, studi ini akan menganalisis dan mensintesis literatur yang ada terkait dengan CBDC untuk mengekstrak informasi kunci yang mendukung perkembangan kerangka desain CBDC Indonesia. ([[.raw/papers/designing-central-bank-digital-currency-in-indonesia-a-critical-literature-review#Page 3|PDF p. 3]]).
- Analisis komparatif juga dilakukan untuk mengeksplorasi kesamaan, perbedaan, dan implikasi potensial dengan membandingkan desain CBDC ritel Indonesia dengan yang diterapkan di negara lain. ([[.raw/papers/designing-central-bank-digital-currency-in-indonesia-a-critical-literature-review#Page 3|PDF p. 3]]).

## Method and evidence

- The Digital Rupiah business model is built in an integrated end-to-end manner based on integration, interoperability, and interconnection aspects (3i). (PDF p. 7).
- Thus, each country must experiment with which CBDC model will suit them the most. (PDF p. 7).

## Limitations and cautions

- The current trajectory of decreasing cash usage, driven by the increasing popularity of digital payments, presents a significant challenge for central banks in fulfilling their crucial roles of preserving financial and monetary stability and ensuring a secure, efficient, and inclusive payment system. (PDF p. 6).
- Currently, the CBDC development in Indonesia is in its preliminary step of considering the benefit and risk analysis of Project Garuda. (PDF p. 7).
