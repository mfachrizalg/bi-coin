- Participants transfer w-Digital Rupiah within the DLT network in accord with best practices that are settlement finality, queue management supported by gridlock resolution, and privacy. ([[.raw/papers/consultative-paper-rupiah-digital-bi#Page 7|PDF p. 7]]).
- B Data Access Arrangements Privacy is an important element of transactions in the wholesale ecosystem. ([[.raw/papers/consultative-paper-rupiah-digital-bi#Page 9|PDF p. 9]]).
- Challenge remains in determining the degree of visibility that could be granted to each party, the selection of optimal privacy-enhancing technology and cryptographic technique, and the balance between confidentiality and auditability. ([[.raw/papers/consultative-paper-rupiah-digital-bi#Page 9|PDF p. 9]]).
- KDR will act as the single-entry point in ensuring security, completeness, validity, and accuracy of Digital Rupiah supply. ([[.raw/papers/consultative-paper-rupiah-digital-bi#Page 11|PDF p. 11]]).
- Bank Indonesia will develop and operate the KDR. ([[.raw/papers/consultative-paper-rupiah-digital-bi#Page 11|PDF p. 11]]).

## Method and evidence

- Lastly, on the final stage (end state), Bank Testing the applicability of the prototype with a sandbox mechanism (using dummy data). (PDF p. 5).
- In a decentralized system, each party can initiate simulation of reprioritization. (PDF p. 12).

## Limitations and cautions

- W-Digital Rupiah would act as a risk-free settlement asset in the wholesale market and become the complement of the central bank reserve accounts. (PDF p. 6).
- Challenge remains in determining the degree of visibility that could be granted to each party, the selection of optimal privacy-enhancing technology and cryptographic technique, and the balance between confidentiality and auditability. (PDF p. 9).
