## Evidence-backed summary

- Benefits of CBDC implementation heavily depend on its design, which, if not executed well, can lead to technological and privacy issues. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-design-choices-guide-for-policymakers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Through case study examination, decision tree analysis, and IT system architecture modelling, this paper identifies key architectural and technological facets of retail CBDCs. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-design-choices-guide-for-policymakers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- We propose a decision-making methodology allowing policymakers to tailor CBDC design to their unique circumstances and requirements. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-design-choices-guide-for-policymakers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- In achieving this, we first outline core and optional CBDC properties and examine design choices from existing projects, form a list of assumptions affecting CBDC architecture and design, as well as vital design choices and tradeoffs. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-design-choices-guide-for-policymakers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Our research includes the development of 36 distinct IT architectures for CBDCs, demonstrating how different assumptions linked to policy objectives can influence the CBDC system design. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-design-choices-guide-for-policymakers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- These findings offer practical guidelines for policymakers, emphasizing the necessity to clearly define and prioritize policy objectives to form correct assumptions before applying our methodology. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-design-choices-guide-for-policymakers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This approach helps to ensure that the CBDC design is optimally aligned with national economic goals and technological capabilities. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-design-choices-guide-for-policymakers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
