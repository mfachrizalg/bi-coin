Official central-bank technical report on the fourth phase of Sweden's e-krona pilot, covering the most methodologically mature offline CBDC design among the nine cases.

## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Documents a four-phase progressive pilot culminating in a 'shadow wallet' architecture: balances stored online while physical cards record up to five offline transactions. Built a permissioned DLT platform capable of running in both connected and disconnected environments, with direct claims to the central bank.
