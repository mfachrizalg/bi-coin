([[.raw/papers/blockchain-in-operations-management-and-manufacturing-potential-and-barriers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Semi-structured interviews with industry experts are employed to elaborate on promising practices for the industry to effi­ ciently promote blockchain adoption and meaningful research directions for scholars. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-in-operations-management-and-manufacturing-potential-and-barriers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Findings include unex­ plored potential regarding distributed production networks and collaboration, expected evolutionary steps of IoT, disintermediation leading to new business models like tokenisation, and short-term rather than long-term relationships. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-in-operations-management-and-manufacturing-potential-and-barriers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Improving smart contract security and interoperability of private and public protocols will enable further dissemination of the technology. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-in-operations-management-and-manufacturing-potential-and-barriers#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, scientific contributions and suc­ cessful industrial applications in this area are still scarce and mainly at a proof-of-concept stage. (PDF p. 1).
- Big data generated from IoT devices can be analysed by connected partners to gain deeper insights into production processes and enhance quality and risk management. (PDF p. 1).
