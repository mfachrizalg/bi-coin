## Supplied study type

Methodological framework paper introducing the three-axis Comparative Case Studies (CCS) approach: horizontal, vertical, and transversal comparison.

## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Proposes a three-level comparative analysis: horizontal (comparison across cases at the same level), vertical (comparison across institutional layers within each case), and transversal (comparison across time/development phases). Designed to address limitations of single- and bilateral-case approaches.
