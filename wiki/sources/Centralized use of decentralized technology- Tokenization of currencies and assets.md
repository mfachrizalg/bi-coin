- Blockchain technology has been adopted in various industries and contexts since Bitcoin was launched (Zhan et al., 2023; Piñeiro-Chousa et al., 2022). ([[.raw/papers/centralized-use-of-decentralized-technology-tokenization-of-currencies-and-assets#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This ongoing trend is termed tokenization, which involves adoption of blockchain technology at organizational, industrial, and national levels. ([[.raw/papers/centralized-use-of-decentralized-technology-tokenization-of-currencies-and-assets#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- However, much literature on blockchain adoption fo­ cuses on the organizational perspective (e.g., Dehghani et al., 2022; Rico-Peña et al., 2023). ([[.raw/papers/centralized-use-of-decentralized-technology-tokenization-of-currencies-and-assets#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Only a few papers take a national perspective in analysis of monetary and financial contexts. ([[.raw/papers/centralized-use-of-decentralized-technology-tokenization-of-currencies-and-assets#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Wang et al. (2022) develop various CBDC-related indices and the negative relationship with the MSCI World Banks Index. ([[.raw/papers/centralized-use-of-decentralized-technology-tokenization-of-currencies-and-assets#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- China is then used as a case study to explore how blockchain technology is adopted to enable central bank digital currency, bond tokenization, and “currency bridge”. (PDF p. 1).
- Based on various contexts analyzed, we extend the Technology Acceptance Model, highlighting the roles of perceived benefits, perceived risks, and collaborative leadership in building trust in and promoting adoption of tokeni­ zation. (PDF p. 1).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, much literature on blockchain adoption fo­ cuses on the organizational perspective (e.g., Dehghani et al., 2022; Rico-Peña et al., 2023). (PDF p. 1).
- Another notorious risk of tokenization is security concerns. (PDF p. 2).
