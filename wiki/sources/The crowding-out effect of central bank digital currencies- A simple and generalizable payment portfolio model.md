## Evidence-backed summary

- Specifically, economic agents choose among cash, deposits, and CBDC to maximize the utility while satisfying their basic payment needs. ([[.raw/papers/the-crowding-out-effect-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-a-simple-and-generalizable-payment-portfolio-model#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Our model shows that, under the certain condition, agents have de­ mand for CBDC and reduce the holdings of cash and deposits at the same time. ([[.raw/papers/the-crowding-out-effect-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-a-simple-and-generalizable-payment-portfolio-model#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The issuance of CBDC is a double-edged sword: the benefit of replacing physical cash for a more efficient payment system comes at the cost of disrupting the deposit base for commercial banks. ([[.raw/papers/the-crowding-out-effect-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-a-simple-and-generalizable-payment-portfolio-model#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- With the emergence of cryptocurrencies and the advances of information technologies, Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDC) have raised increasing attentions of both policymakers and researchers. ([[.raw/papers/the-crowding-out-effect-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-a-simple-and-generalizable-payment-portfolio-model#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- While there is a growing body of literature on private digital currencies such as Bitcoin and stablecoin (Baur and Hoang, 2021; Kyriazis and Prassa, 2019; Makarov and Schoar, 2020), the research on CBDC is relatively scarce, the majority of which comes from international organizations and central banks (Auer et al., 2020; Kumhof and Noone, 2018). ([[.raw/papers/the-crowding-out-effect-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-a-simple-and-generalizable-payment-portfolio-model#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The CBDC is a digital form of central bank money that is different from physical cash or digital central bank reserves/settlement accounts. ([[.raw/papers/the-crowding-out-effect-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-a-simple-and-generalizable-payment-portfolio-model#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Based on the accessibility, CBDC can be further categorized into wholesale CBDC and general purpose CBDC (CPMI-MC, 2018). ([[.raw/papers/the-crowding-out-effect-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-a-simple-and-generalizable-payment-portfolio-model#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- Our model shows that, under the certain condition, agents have de­ mand for CBDC and reduce the holdings of cash and deposits at the same time. (PDF p. 1).
- Keywords: CBDC Payment portfolio model Crowding-out effect 1. (PDF p. 1).

## Limitations and cautions

- Note that, in practice, many policymakers seem to underestimate the potential crowding-out effect of CBDC on deposits over the course of its substitution for cash.1 However, the argument that CBDC would simply replace physical cash without crowding out deposits is not justified. (PDF p. 1).
- However, without the existence of CBDC, the unique features of cash include not only anonymity, but also legal tender. (PDF p. 3).
