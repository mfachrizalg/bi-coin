Official central-bank technical report on Thailand's Project Inthanon CBDC, covering both the wholesale Corda-based architecture and the migration toward a centralized retail platform.

## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Documents successful wholesale CBDC implementation using R3 Corda for Inthanon Wholesale. Reports the pragmatic decision to migrate to centralized G+D Filia for retail after Corda proved inadequate at retail throughput, illustrating independent convergence toward centralized retail architectures.
