## Method and evidence

- Email: solikin@bi.go.id ABSTRACT This paper attempts to explain the differences in Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) adoption across emerging and advanced countries using an ordered probit model. (PDF p. 2).
- Based on a cross-country dataset, we show that wholesale CBDC is more advanced in countries with developed financial markets and greater cross-border transactions. (PDF p. 2).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, cross-border transactions are the most crucial factor influencing wholesale CBDC adoption across emerging and advanced countries. (PDF p. 2).
- Digital transactions have experienced more accelerated growth over the last decade and more so during the COVID-19 pandemic, since they do not require direct contact, thus reducing the risk of transmitting the virus (Alfonso et al., 2021). (PDF p. 3).
