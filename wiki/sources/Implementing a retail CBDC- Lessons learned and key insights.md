# Implementing a retail CBDC: Lessons learned and key insights

## Evidence-backed summary

- Kitts and Nevis c a b s t r a c t Motivations for introducing a retail central bank digital currency (CBDC) could range from a rapid decline in the use of physical cash or ﬁnancial inclusion strategies to an underdeveloped retail payment market. ([[.raw/papers/implementing-a-retail-cbdc-lessons-learned-and-key-insights#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Notably, despite diﬀerences among jurisdictions and CBDC pilots, a series of key insights emerge from these experiences that could be useful for those thinking to launch a rCBDC. ([[.raw/papers/implementing-a-retail-cbdc-lessons-learned-and-key-insights#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Motivations for central bank digital currencies (rCBDCs), especially in emerging markets and developing economies (EMDEs), range from major eﬃciency gaps in the domestic payment infrastructure to broadening ﬁnancial inclusion (CEMLA, 2019; Mancini-Griﬀoli et al., 2018). ([[.raw/papers/implementing-a-retail-cbdc-lessons-learned-and-key-insights#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- At the same time, rCBDC has become fertile soil to test whether decentralized ledgers and new technologies could help modernize traditional payment infrastructure1 ✩ Our work is the result of the activities of the Central Bank Digital Currencies Working Group (CBDC WG) hosted by the Center for Latin American Monetary Studies (CEMLA). ([[.raw/papers/implementing-a-retail-cbdc-lessons-learned-and-key-insights#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- In all cases, a thorough and cautious analysis has taken place before embarking on an implementation/testing plan, especially regarding the motivations and operationalization of such projects. ([[.raw/papers/implementing-a-retail-cbdc-lessons-learned-and-key-insights#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- As can be expected, the starting conditions and objectives for CBDC pilots diﬀered among jurisdictions. ([[.raw/papers/implementing-a-retail-cbdc-lessons-learned-and-key-insights#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- Above all, central banks are guided by foundational principles related to how rCBDC systems may be intended to become public–private partnerships, aspiring to supplement cash and fulﬁll a wide range of population needs, not endangering the capacity of the central bank to safeguard its overarching objectives of monetary and ﬁnancial stability (Bordo and Levin, 2017; Kahn et al., 2019). ([[.raw/papers/implementing-a-retail-cbdc-lessons-learned-and-key-insights#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, the scale of the pilot programs and the evolving nature of these technologies force policymakers to carefully approach such new technologies. (PDF p. 2).
- Interestingly, the CPMI-IOSCO Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures (PFMI) are a powerful device to assess the operational, governance, access and business management, and ﬁnancial risk management of a rCBDC (Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures, 2012). (PDF p. 2).
