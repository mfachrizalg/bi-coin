## Supplied study type

Cross-platform comparative evaluation of major DLT platforms for CBDC use, assessing each across multiple design dimensions.

## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Evaluates Hyperledger Fabric, Hyperledger Besu, R3 Corda, and Ethereum variants across privacy, scalability, programmability, and regulatory compliance. Finds that no single platform simultaneously excels across all CBDC technology components. Real-world implementations (Brazil's Besu issues, Thailand's Corda-to-centralized migration, India's decision to skip DLT for retail) confirm these theoretical predictions, though in more dramatic form than anticipated.
