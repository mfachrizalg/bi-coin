## Evidence-backed summary

- This diploma thesis is aimed at describing and comparing four open-source blockchain technologies - Ethereum and Hyperledger’s blockchains - Fabric, Besu, and Iroha. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-open-source-software-comparison#Page 8|PDF p. 8]]).
- The goal of this thesis is to provide a comprehensive comparison of the functionality and performance of the selected blockchains, and evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of each blockchain. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-open-source-software-comparison#Page 8|PDF p. 8]]).
- The thesis will describe what a blockchain technology is, on what principles is it built, what are the data structures used and the most common attack aimed at blockchains. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-open-source-software-comparison#Page 8|PDF p. 8]]).
- Next, the thesis outlines the inner-workings of each blockchain how are they implemented from a high-level point-of-view, how are transactions executed and how is the consensus achieved. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-open-source-software-comparison#Page 8|PDF p. 8]]).
- Later, the thesis will run test cases for performance benchmarking and collect statistical data for analysis and comparison. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-open-source-software-comparison#Page 8|PDF p. 8]]).
- Lastly, the thesis will compare the blockchains and discuss the advantages and disadvantages of each tested blockchain. iv ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-open-source-software-comparison#Page 8|PDF p. 8]]).
