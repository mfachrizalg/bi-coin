## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Finds that technical readiness alone is insufficient for CBDC adoption: user incentives, merchant acceptance, and interoperability with existing payment systems are more decisive predictors of adoption than architectural choices. Reports Sand Dollar circulation at less than 0.5%.
