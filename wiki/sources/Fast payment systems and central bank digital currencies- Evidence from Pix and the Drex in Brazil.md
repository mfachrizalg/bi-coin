([[.raw/papers/fast-payment-systems-and-central-bank-digital-currencies-evidence-from-pix-and-the-drex-in-brazil#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Findings suggest that connectivity and securities issues are the most significant challenges for further adoption of Pix. ([[.raw/papers/fast-payment-systems-and-central-bank-digital-currencies-evidence-from-pix-and-the-drex-in-brazil#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- A well-designed CBDC system could address the challenges of failed transactions due to connectivity and security issues, if it is designed specifically considering these shortcomings as priorities. ([[.raw/papers/fast-payment-systems-and-central-bank-digital-currencies-evidence-from-pix-and-the-drex-in-brazil#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- By January 2026, there were three live retail CBDCs implemented globally in Jamaica, Bahamas, and Nigeria while 11 central banks were conducting retail CBDC pilots (CBDC Tracker). ([[.raw/papers/fast-payment-systems-and-central-bank-digital-currencies-evidence-from-pix-and-the-drex-in-brazil#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- A CBDC is a form of digssital money, denominated in the national unit of account, which is a direct liability of the central bank (BIS, 2021). ([[.raw/papers/fast-payment-systems-and-central-bank-digital-currencies-evidence-from-pix-and-the-drex-in-brazil#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- In the central banking community, there is a certain level of acknowledgement and understanding of the potential advantages of having a CBDC system. ([[.raw/papers/fast-payment-systems-and-central-bank-digital-currencies-evidence-from-pix-and-the-drex-in-brazil#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- This paper assesses the value proposition of a CBDC system in comparison to current payments schemes such as Pix in Brazil, based on quantitative and qualitative data collected through a survey of a sample of one thousand adults and merchants and focus groups in urban, peri‑urban and rural areas in Brazil. (PDF p. 1).
- It synthesizes selected data and insights gathered from October to November 2024 through a survey with a sample of one thousand individuals and merchants and focus groups conducted in Itatiba and Mococa in the State of Saõ Paolo. (PDF p. 1).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, there is no consensus on the benefits to pursue the development of a retail CBDC system which mostly depends on the local context (Fig. (PDF p. 1).
- The cards scheme allowed for digital payments; however, generally, with a deferred and not real-time settlement and at high cost for merchants accepting payments, specifically, micro and small retailers. (PDF p. 2).
