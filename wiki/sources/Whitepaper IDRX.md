## Evidence-backed summary

- As a cryptocurrency, IDRX offers users the benefits of blockchain technology, including security, transparency, and decentralized control. ([[.raw/papers/whitepaper-idrx#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- At the same time, as a stable token, IDRX offers price stability by maintaining a fixed exchange rate with the IDR, which is the national currency of Indonesia. ([[.raw/papers/whitepaper-idrx#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- IDRX aims to facilitate the adoption of cryptocurrencies in Indonesia, while also providing a stable and secure alternative to traditional fiat currencies. ([[.raw/papers/whitepaper-idrx#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- However, the project team recognizes the importance of interoperability and plans to add support for other blockchain networks in the future, as the project continues to develop. ([[.raw/papers/whitepaper-idrx#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Overall, IDRX represents an innovative and exciting approach to stablecoin design, combining the stability of a fiat currency peg with the benefits of blockchain technology. ([[.raw/papers/whitepaper-idrx#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
