## Evidence-backed summary

- This paper represents the opinions of the authors, and is the product of professional research. ([[.raw/papers/monetary-policy-strategy-in-the-presence-of-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Monetary Policy Strategy in the Presence of Central Bank Digital Currency Ferry Syarifuddin Toni Bakhtiar ABSTRACT With their various motivations, many central banks still develop CBDC to explore its potentials and the drawback of implementation. ([[.raw/papers/monetary-policy-strategy-in-the-presence-of-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- This research examines the macroeconomic and monetary policy consequences, then determine optimal CBDC design to support monetary policy strategy. ([[.raw/papers/monetary-policy-strategy-in-the-presence-of-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- First, this research wants to develop DSGE model to quantify macroeconomic and monetary policy consequences in implementing CBDC. ([[.raw/papers/monetary-policy-strategy-in-the-presence-of-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- Second, as we know the outcome of the consequences, we continue to determine optimal CBDC design using SWOT with purposive sampling meta-analysis approach and its implementation strategies. ([[.raw/papers/monetary-policy-strategy-in-the-presence-of-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- According to the simulation, CBDC could effectively maintain inflation through CBDC rate. ([[.raw/papers/monetary-policy-strategy-in-the-presence-of-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- Meanwhile, optimal CBDC design that could support monetary policy is retail and wholesale coverage, interest bearing (wholesale) and non-interest bearing (retail) remuneration, account-based and tokenbased payment system, traceable degree of anonymity, hybrid architecture, DLT ledger system, and domestic and cross-border scope Key words: CBDC, Optimal design, DSGE, SWOT, Monetary policy JEL Classification: E42, E44,  ([[.raw/papers/monetary-policy-strategy-in-the-presence-of-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).

## Method and evidence

- First, this research wants to develop DSGE model to quantify macroeconomic and monetary policy consequences in implementing CBDC. (PDF p. 2).
- The DSGE model is consist of seven sectors namely households, retail firms, wholesale firms, capital producing firms, banks, central bank, and government. (PDF p. 2).
