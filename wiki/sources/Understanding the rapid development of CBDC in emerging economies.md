## Evidence-backed summary

- This study examines the factors influencing CBDC adoption, highlighting the roles of regulatory quality and digital financial access. ([[.raw/papers/understanding-the-rapid-development-of-cbdc-in-emerging-economies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Results indicate that regulatory frameworks, rather than the level of digital infrastructure, predominantly drive CBDC initiatives. ([[.raw/papers/understanding-the-rapid-development-of-cbdc-in-emerging-economies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The findings caution against the rapid deployment of CBDCs in less prepared environments, which could pose risks to financial stability. ([[.raw/papers/understanding-the-rapid-development-of-cbdc-in-emerging-economies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The research underscores the importance of comprehensive regulatory strategies over technological readiness for successful CBDC implementation. ([[.raw/papers/understanding-the-rapid-development-of-cbdc-in-emerging-economies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Regions with below-average technological availability will remain disadvantaged; therefore, successful CBDC implementation must coincide with simultaneous structural advancement. ([[.raw/papers/understanding-the-rapid-development-of-cbdc-in-emerging-economies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Corbet et al. to more readily adopt CBDCs, highlighting the successful CBDC implementations in diverse monetary environments such as the Bahamas and Uruguay, which suggest that both economic maturity and regulatory frameworks play pivotal roles in CBDC adoption. ([[.raw/papers/understanding-the-rapid-development-of-cbdc-in-emerging-economies#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- Similarly, Alonso et al. (2020) emphasises the significance of regulatory quality and financial inclusion as determinants for adopting CBDCs, illustrating that countries with robust regulatory frameworks and high levels of digital financial access are better positioned to implement and manage CBDCs effectively. ([[.raw/papers/understanding-the-rapid-development-of-cbdc-in-emerging-economies#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).

## Method and evidence

- Further, we incorporate comparative measures of regulatory quality using sources such as the IMF and World Bank to ensure the largest breadth of data.6 We utilise a panel regression methodology to analyse the evolution of CBDCs across eighty-five countries from January 2015 to June 2023, generating a dataset that includes 680 annual observations. (PDF p. 3).
- Our regression model is detailed to reflect the nuances of these variables over time, providing a robust analysis of the factors that drive or hinder the advancement of CBDCs across different national contexts. (PDF p. 3).
