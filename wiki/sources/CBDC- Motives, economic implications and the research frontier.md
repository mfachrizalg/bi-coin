## Supplied study type

Cross-country survey research on CBDC motivations across 81 central banks, with a synthesis of economic implications and a mapping of the open research agenda.

## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Identifies systematic divergence in CBDC motivation: developed economies prioritize payment efficiency and monetary policy transmission, while developing economies emphasize financial inclusion and payment system resilience. Suggests motivational divergence shapes technology architecture, with inclusion-driven CBDCs adopting simpler platforms and efficiency-driven CBDCs adopting more complex layered architectures.
