## Evidence-backed summary

- Few countries have launched the CBDC or widely used it in the economy. ([[.raw/papers/adoption-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-initial-evidence-from-china#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- In addition to surveying the literature, which features extremely scarce empirical studies due to data limitations, this paper provides likely the earliest and the most comprehensive empirical documentation of the adoption of China’s CBDC (e-CNY) after the central bank launched and actively promoted it in pilot regions using both regulatory power and economic incentives. ([[.raw/papers/adoption-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-initial-evidence-from-china#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- We find that regions with active promotions for e-CNY plausibly witness more frequent and larger e-CNY transactions, more wallet creations, and greater merchant adoption. ([[.raw/papers/adoption-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-initial-evidence-from-china#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Central banks all over the world are actively exploring central bank digital currencies (CBDCs), but few countries have launched CBDC as a legal means of payment. ([[.raw/papers/adoption-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-initial-evidence-from-china#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- CBDC refers to the digital version of a country’s fiat currency, but in different forms and with different roles as set out by each country (Cong et al., 2024). ([[.raw/papers/adoption-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-initial-evidence-from-china#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- According to the CBDC survey results released by the Bank for International Settlements in July 2023, 93 % of the 86 surveyed central banks have engaged in some form of CBDC work and 25 % are piloting a retail CBDC. ([[.raw/papers/adoption-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-initial-evidence-from-china#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- China is a pioneer in exploring CBDC with an early-stage research starting in 2014. ([[.raw/papers/adoption-of-central-bank-digital-currencies-initial-evidence-from-china#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- Given the world eagerly learns from China’s experiment and China plans to expand the application scope of e-CNY as a general payment tool domestically and internationally with added smart-contract functions, we discuss primary challenges and potential paths forward for the development of e-CNY and CBDCs in general. (PDF p. 1).
- According to the CBDC survey results released by the Bank for International Settlements in July 2023, 93 % of the 86 surveyed central banks have engaged in some form of CBDC work and 25 % are piloting a retail CBDC. (PDF p. 1).

## Limitations and cautions

- In addition to surveying the literature, which features extremely scarce empirical studies due to data limitations, this paper provides likely the earliest and the most comprehensive empirical documentation of the adoption of China’s CBDC (e-CNY) after the central bank launched and actively promoted it in pilot regions using both regulatory power and economic incentives. (PDF p. 1).
- However, despite the strong intervention, individual users mostly stick to existing electronic payment Apps and are reluctant to switch to eCNY. (PDF p. 1).
