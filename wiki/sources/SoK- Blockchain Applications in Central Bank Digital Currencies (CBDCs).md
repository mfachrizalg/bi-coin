## Evidence-backed summary

- Blockchain or Distributed Ledger technology (DLT) can be used to implement CBDC to execute and settle peer-to-peer transactions. ([[.raw/papers/sok-blockchain-applications-in-central-bank-digital-currencies-cbdcs#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- With the emergence of private money, such as cryptocurrencies and stablecoins, and the growing use of digital payments to lessen the global pandemic spread, CBDC is an active research area among central banks worldwide. ([[.raw/papers/sok-blockchain-applications-in-central-bank-digital-currencies-cbdcs#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Many central banks started their CBDC projects by building DLT proofs of concept (PoCs) to replicate wholesale payment systems and expand their investigation into other use cases, such as delivery versus Payment (DvP) and cross-border remittance. ([[.raw/papers/sok-blockchain-applications-in-central-bank-digital-currencies-cbdcs#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Many large economies like the United States have projects exploring CBDC. ([[.raw/papers/sok-blockchain-applications-in-central-bank-digital-currencies-cbdcs#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This paper discusses the application of blockchain for CBDC by presenting CBDC projects by central banks. ([[.raw/papers/sok-blockchain-applications-in-central-bank-digital-currencies-cbdcs#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Moreover, this paper analyses issues, identify challenges and discusses future works in this rapidly evolving field. ([[.raw/papers/sok-blockchain-applications-in-central-bank-digital-currencies-cbdcs#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The second subsection describes the organization and contribution of this paper. ([[.raw/papers/sok-blockchain-applications-in-central-bank-digital-currencies-cbdcs#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- People cannot store the value of goods for a long time since most goods traded using this method are perishable. (PDF p. 1).
- Utilizing CBDCs can help financial institutions or fintech companies experiment with DLT on its programable money features, encouraging competition and innovation in the financial sector. (PDF p. 3).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, this arrangement takes time and suffers at least three significant issues. (PDF p. 1).
- However, those traded items used as currencies were similar but not identical in every unit. (PDF p. 1).
