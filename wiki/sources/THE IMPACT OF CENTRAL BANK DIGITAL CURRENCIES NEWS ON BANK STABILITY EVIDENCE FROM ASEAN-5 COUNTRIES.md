## Method and evidence

- Employing the System Generalized Method of Moments (GMM) approach with data from 92 banks across ASEAN-5 nations, and implementing quantile regression for robustness checks, the findings demonstrate a significant relationship between the CBDCAI and bank stability, alongside variables such as bank size and capitalization. (PDF p. 2).

## Limitations and cautions

- The perception of risk and stability may be altered by CBDCs, which are viewed differently from traditional currencies and assets. (PDF p. 3).
- However, not all digital money is increasingly used, although there are various concerns including security and risk over the absence of jurisdictional guarantees and its volatile nature makes this innovation even difficult to gain public trust (Fadli et al., 2023). (PDF p. 5).
