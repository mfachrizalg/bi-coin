## Evidence-backed summary

- This study explores blockchain adoption in retail e-commerce, focusing on its potential to enhance efficiency and security. ([[.raw/papers/indonesian-peoples-readiness-for-blockchain-adoption-in-e-commerce#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- It examines the use of blockchain, particularly Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC), which uses a private blockchain regulated by central authorities, unlike public cryptocurrencies. ([[.raw/papers/indonesian-peoples-readiness-for-blockchain-adoption-in-e-commerce#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Using the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), the study assesses public readiness for blockchain adoption through online questionnaires distributed to Indonesian e-commerce users. ([[.raw/papers/indonesian-peoples-readiness-for-blockchain-adoption-in-e-commerce#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The results highlight key factors such as security, transparency, cost efficiency, and transaction speed that significantly influence consumer trust and adoption. ([[.raw/papers/indonesian-peoples-readiness-for-blockchain-adoption-in-e-commerce#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The study also identifies regulatory challenges and infrastructural readiness as crucial aspects in the adoption process. ([[.raw/papers/indonesian-peoples-readiness-for-blockchain-adoption-in-e-commerce#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Furthermore, this research offers practical recommendations for policymakers, industry leaders, and regulators to enhance digital infrastructure, improve cybersecurity measures, and establish a regulatory framework for secure blockchain adoption. ([[.raw/papers/indonesian-peoples-readiness-for-blockchain-adoption-in-e-commerce#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- In contrast, blockchain eliminates intermediaries through peer-to-peer networks, resulting in cost savings, enhanced security via cryptography, and greater transparency with immutable records. ([[.raw/papers/indonesian-peoples-readiness-for-blockchain-adoption-in-e-commerce#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- Using the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), the study assesses public readiness for blockchain adoption through online questionnaires distributed to Indonesian e-commerce users. (PDF p. 1).
- Keywords— blockchain, central bank digital currency, ecommerce, technology acceptance model, transaction However, Indonesia faces regulatory barriers to blockchain adoption as a payment method. (PDF p. 1).

## Limitations and cautions

- Keywords— blockchain, central bank digital currency, ecommerce, technology acceptance model, transaction However, Indonesia faces regulatory barriers to blockchain adoption as a payment method. (PDF p. 1).
- To navigate this limitation, the Central Bank of Indonesia has initiated the Garuda Project, which explores the development of a Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) called the Digital Rupiah [2]. (PDF p. 1).
