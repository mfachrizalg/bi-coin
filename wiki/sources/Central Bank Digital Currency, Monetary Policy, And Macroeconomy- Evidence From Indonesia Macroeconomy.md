## Evidence-backed summary

- It has been accepted for inclusion in Bulletin of Monetary Economics and Banking by an authorized editor of Bulletin of Monetary Economics and Banking. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-monetary-policy-and-macroeconomy-evidence-from-indonesia-macroeconomy#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This paper studies the impact of a universally accessible interest-bearing Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) on macroeconomic stability and monetary policy in Indonesia. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-monetary-policy-and-macroeconomy-evidence-from-indonesia-macroeconomy#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- We find that the CBDC regime significantly improves macroeconomic stability in Indonesia. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-monetary-policy-and-macroeconomy-evidence-from-indonesia-macroeconomy#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- In this regard, the CBDC rate, as an additional monetary policy instrument, responsively reacts to macroeconomic fluctuations and significantly impacts the demand side. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-monetary-policy-and-macroeconomy-evidence-from-indonesia-macroeconomy#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- Our results also suggest that monetary policy is more effective under the CBDC regime than under the non-CBDC. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-monetary-policy-and-macroeconomy-evidence-from-indonesia-macroeconomy#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).
- The governor of the Central Bank of Indonesia (Bank Indonesia hereafter), Perry Warjiyo, has made a public statement about the intention of Bank Indonesia to issue Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC hereafter) in the future (Reuters, 2021). ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-monetary-policy-and-macroeconomy-evidence-from-indonesia-macroeconomy#Page 3|PDF p. 3]]).
- With an intention to accelerate the issuance of CBDC, Bank Indonesia now prioritizes the digital payment advancement policy, supporting payment system digitalization. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-monetary-policy-and-macroeconomy-evidence-from-indonesia-macroeconomy#Page 3|PDF p. 3]]).

## Method and evidence

- We construct a simple reduced-form New Keynesian model calibrated with monthly data for Indonesia from July 2005 to February 2022. (PDF p. 2).
- By using the Computational General Equilibrium (CGE) simulation, they find that the issuance of CBDC will initially increase the deposit rate, increases national and regional economic growth, and increases the employment rate. (PDF p. 4).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, none of their findings address the macroeconomic stability impact of the CBDC in Indonesia. (PDF p. 4).
- However, there is a lot of room for exploration that this paper has not mentioned in detail. (PDF p. 5).
