## Evidence-backed summary

- The CBDC utilizes digital tokens to indicate any nation’s fiat/stable currency that manages under distributed ledger technology such as blockchain. ([[.raw/papers/permission-blockchain-network-based-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Compared to existing cryptocurrencies, the CBDC is regulated by the government monetary policies, including the issuance of digital tokens (i.e., digitized central bank money) and circulation of these digital tokens to the commercial banks, then to the public/end-users. ([[.raw/papers/permission-blockchain-network-based-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This paper proposes a two-layered CBDC architecture after analyzing the recent research and existing CBDC pilot projects. ([[.raw/papers/permission-blockchain-network-based-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The distribution layer shows wholesale CBDC based on permission blockchain network (PBN) between the central bank and the commercial banks. ([[.raw/papers/permission-blockchain-network-based-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- While, the user layer describes the functioning of retail CBDC, which shows the interaction between the commercial banks and the end-users through the token-based account. ([[.raw/papers/permission-blockchain-network-based-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- We aim to provide the CBDC design architecture and smart-contract functions for PBN. address current financial infrastructure vulnerabilities and inefficiencies by introducing decentralized monetary policies, a distributed secure payment system, reducing counterparty risks, stabilizing the financial payment market, etc. ([[.raw/papers/permission-blockchain-network-based-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- There exist two types of CBDCs such as wholesale CBDC and retail CBDC. ([[.raw/papers/permission-blockchain-network-based-central-bank-digital-currency#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- The Bank of Bahamas released its public document on CBDC in 2017 with the CBDC project name Sand Dollar and selected exuma island to experiment with the digital Bahamian dollar in the retail and wholesale domain [15]. entire lifecycle of CBDC from issuance to withdrawal of digital currency with an example of cross-border payment to describe the transaction payment and settlement through CBDC. (PDF p. 2).
- On the other side, the disadvantage of having this model is the tremendous workload. (PDF p. 2).

## Limitations and cautions

- The benefit of bringing wholesale CBDC in place of the traditional wholesale market is enhancing security and payment settlement efficiency, enabling real-time monitoring, minimizing counterparty credits and liquidity risk. (PDF p. 1).
- Finally, concluding remarks and future work is given in Section V. $ Central Bank End-user1 II. (PDF p. 2).
