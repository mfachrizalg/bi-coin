# Central bank digital currency and systemic risk

- Original: `thesis/reference/Central bank digital currency and systemic risk.pdf`

## Evidence-backed summary

- Several countries are involved in CBDC development at different stages and a few are already in the launching stage. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-and-systemic-risk#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- We use the autoregressive distributed lag approach to explore the association between CBDC-related news and systemic risk in the short and long run by employing dynamic panel heterogeneity analysis. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-and-systemic-risk#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The results show that CBDC-related news has a significant negative association with systemic risk in the long run, indicating a positive reception by the global financial sector. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-and-systemic-risk#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Extended analysis shows that the long-run negative association is consistent across different income levels and geographical regions. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-and-systemic-risk#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- However, countries in the advanced stages of CBDC development show a significant positive association between CBDC-related news and systemic risk warranting the utmost care in implementing CBDC initiatives. ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-and-systemic-risk#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Over the past ten years, decentralized finance (DeFi) and financial technology (FinTech) have enhanced financial inclusion and permeated every aspect of the financial system (Fan et al., 2023; Awais et al., 2023; Zarrouk et al., 2021; Zhou et al., 2022). ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-and-systemic-risk#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Due to these developments in technology-oriented payment systems, central banks of many countries are actively working on issuing digital currencies, widely known as Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) (Hoang et al., 2023; Bech et al., 2017). ([[.raw/papers/central-bank-digital-currency-and-systemic-risk#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
