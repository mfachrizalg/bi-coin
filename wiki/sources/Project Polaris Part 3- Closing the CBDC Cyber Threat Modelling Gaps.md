## Evidence-backed summary

- Project Polaris Part 3: Closing the CBDC cyber threat modelling gaps July 2023 In parternship with: BIS Cyber Resilience Coordination Centre ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-3-closing-the-cbdc-cyber-threat-modelling-gaps#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Closing the CBDC cyber threat modeling gaps 1. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-3-closing-the-cbdc-cyber-threat-modelling-gaps#Page 4|PDF p. 4]]).
- It has been argued that DeFi started in 2009 with the launch of Bitcoin, the first peer-topeer digital asset to use blockchain technology. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-3-closing-the-cbdc-cyber-threat-modelling-gaps#Page 4|PDF p. 4]]).
- 1 DeFi can be defined as an umbrella term for an eclectic mix of blockchain technology, digital assets, decentralised applications (dApps), and distributed ledgers (DLT). ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-3-closing-the-cbdc-cyber-threat-modelling-gaps#Page 4|PDF p. 4]]).
- Cryptocurrencies in particular have enjoyed a remarkable adoption rate due to their accessibility and low transaction fees, all without the need for intermediaries as in the case of traditional banks. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-3-closing-the-cbdc-cyber-threat-modelling-gaps#Page 4|PDF p. 4]]).
- Perhaps most worrisome are the known and unknown security vulnerabilities, which are unique to this ecosystem and stem from the use of novel technology and the lack of verified secure designs and implementations. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-3-closing-the-cbdc-cyber-threat-modelling-gaps#Page 4|PDF p. 4]]).
- CBDCs offer the promise of a more secure and stable digital currency that could also support financial inclusion and reduce cash usage whilst allowing for more efficient, faster, and cheaper transactions, as compared with traditional banking systems. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-3-closing-the-cbdc-cyber-threat-modelling-gaps#Page 4|PDF p. 4]]).

## Method and evidence

- Specifically, although the majority of existing TTPs could be used to model the attacks, some will require slight modification, while there exist new attack vectors that do not fit within the framework and will necessitate the creation of new TTPs. (PDF p. 5).
- Examples of new TTPs that could be used to model the novel attacks are provided and the use of crowdsourcing is proposed to further analyse how attacks against CBDCs that use DLT as part of their reference architecture can be adequately modelled using the MITRE ATT&CK framework. (PDF p. 5).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, cryptocurrencies can be volatile, often lacking coherent regulations, governance, and government support. (PDF p. 4).
- However, there have been many high-profile cyber attacks in the DeFi domain, eg exploiting weaknesses in consensus mechanisms as well as smart contracts that enable attacks on cryptocurrency exchanges and wallets. (PDF p. 4).
