# The determinants of issuing central bank digital currencies

## Evidence-backed summary

- This study aims to identify factors that influence central banks’ decisions in taking advanced actions to issue CBDC, namely, the economic, market, demographic and technical factors. ([[.raw/papers/the-determinants-of-issuing-central-bank-digital-currencies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Data is collected from the CBDC Tracker and the WB database for the period 2013–2021. ([[.raw/papers/the-determinants-of-issuing-central-bank-digital-currencies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- We applied the Pooled OLS es­ timations to examine the impact of the key factors on being in an advanced stage for issuing CBDC; moreover, probit and logistic regression are employed to robust our results and overcome the limitations of Pooled OLS. ([[.raw/papers/the-determinants-of-issuing-central-bank-digital-currencies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The findings demonstrate that underdeveloped economies are more engaged in issuing CBDC. ([[.raw/papers/the-determinants-of-issuing-central-bank-digital-currencies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Besides, better regulations, FDI inflow, young populations, and more urban societies would increase the probability of CBDC issuance. ([[.raw/papers/the-determinants-of-issuing-central-bank-digital-currencies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Nevertheless, results show the impact of technical factors is heterogeneous across countries. ([[.raw/papers/the-determinants-of-issuing-central-bank-digital-currencies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- It has also been argued that, in the digital age, the growth of cashless payments and the rise of cryptocurrencies are not only challenging central banks’ monetary prerogatives but also posing new threats to the stability and integrity of the financial system (Perret, 2019). ([[.raw/papers/the-determinants-of-issuing-central-bank-digital-currencies#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- To achieve the objectives of this study, we employed three approaches: a linear regression model, probit model and logistic regression. (PDF p. 2).
- First, as one of the novel studies in this area, we contribute to building an econometric model for the determinants of CBDC for central banks to examine before launching CBDC. (PDF p. 2).

## Limitations and cautions

- We applied the Pooled OLS es­ timations to examine the impact of the key factors on being in an advanced stage for issuing CBDC; moreover, probit and logistic regression are employed to robust our results and overcome the limitations of Pooled OLS. (PDF p. 1).
- However, as cash use declined, faster and more convenient digital payments have emerged and grown in volume and scope all over the world. (PDF p. 1).
