## Evidence-backed summary

- This paper introduces the Smart Reserve Vault (SRV), a conceptual framework anchoring retail CBDC issuance to tokenized strategic reserves via auditable smart-contract rules. ([[.raw/papers/smart-reserve-vault-a-conceptual-model-for-issuance-and-burning-of-digital-rupiah#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Using a mixed qualitativedesign science approach, we synthesized comparative CBDC evidence and thematic analysis of n=9 expert interviews. ([[.raw/papers/smart-reserve-vault-a-conceptual-model-for-issuance-and-burning-of-digital-rupiah#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The findings formalize the SRV artifact, detailing (i) quota-based minting derived from marked-to-market reserve value and (ii) trigger-based burning governed by macro-financial thresholds with circuit-breaker safeguards. ([[.raw/papers/smart-reserve-vault-a-conceptual-model-for-issuance-and-burning-of-digital-rupiah#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- An Expert-Validated Design: We validate the framework's core components and two-tier compatibility using thematic analysis from n=9 targeted expert interviews. ([[.raw/papers/smart-reserve-vault-a-conceptual-model-for-issuance-and-burning-of-digital-rupiah#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- While designs favor two-tier structures to mitigate disintermediation [3], [4], [6], [7], a critical gap remains. landscape; Existing work clarifies CBDC objectives but lacks operational rules linking tokenized reserves to programmable supply adjustments [1], [2], [3], [4], [5]. ([[.raw/papers/smart-reserve-vault-a-conceptual-model-for-issuance-and-burning-of-digital-rupiah#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This paper addresses this gap by proposing the Smart Reserve Vault (SRV): a two-tier-compatible mechanism anchoring Digital Rupiah issuance and burning to tokenized strategic reserves (e.g., FX, gold) via auditable smart-contract rules. ([[.raw/papers/smart-reserve-vault-a-conceptual-model-for-issuance-and-burning-of-digital-rupiah#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This research makes three primary contributions: 1. ([[.raw/papers/smart-reserve-vault-a-conceptual-model-for-issuance-and-burning-of-digital-rupiah#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- This two-tier compatible model offers a pathway to strengthen supply discipline and exchangerate credibility without disintermediating banks. (PDF p. 1).
- This work provides an auditable technical model for central banks seeking to enhance public trust. (PDF p. 1).

## Limitations and cautions

- The primary limitation is the conceptual nature of the evaluation, which lacks live monetary deployment data. (PDF p. 1).
- However, many current pilots lack operational transparency, failing to specify the rules linking digital currency supply to a central bank's reserve assets. (PDF p. 1).
