([[.raw/papers/user-adoption-of-digital-currency-a-systematic-review-and-future-agenda-using-tccm-approach#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

- A digital banknote, known as a retail CBDC, can be utilized by individuals to make payments to merchants, retailers, or other individuals. ([[.raw/papers/user-adoption-of-digital-currency-a-systematic-review-and-future-agenda-using-tccm-approach#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
