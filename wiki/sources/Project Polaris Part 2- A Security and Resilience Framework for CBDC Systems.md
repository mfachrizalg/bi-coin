## Evidence-backed summary

- Project Polaris Part 2: A security and resilience framework for CBDC systems July 2023 In parternship with: BIS Cyber Resilience Coordination Centre ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-2-a-security-and-resilience-framework-for-cbdc-systems#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- A security and resilience framework for CBDC systems 1. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-2-a-security-and-resilience-framework-for-cbdc-systems#Page 5|PDF p. 5]]).
- 1 Central bank digital currency (CBDC) systems would be considered a critical national infrastructure, much like real-time gross settlement (RTGS) systems are today. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-2-a-security-and-resilience-framework-for-cbdc-systems#Page 5|PDF p. 5]]).
- CBDC systems could use new technologies, some of which are as yet unproven at the scale and critical operations demands that would be required, and therefore could introduce new security and operational risks. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-2-a-security-and-resilience-framework-for-cbdc-systems#Page 5|PDF p. 5]]).
- Central banks and other actors in a CBDC ecosystem 4 will need to face up to this increasingly complex cyber threat landscape, comprised of unpredictable threat actors, new threats, a large attack surface and points of failure, supply chain risks and an environment where the potential upside for a threat actor could be high. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-2-a-security-and-resilience-framework-for-cbdc-systems#Page 5|PDF p. 5]]).
- See Doerr et al (2022) for survey results on cyber threats and central banking. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-2-a-security-and-resilience-framework-for-cbdc-systems#Page 5|PDF p. 5]]).
- A CBDC ecosystem refers to the various public and private sector actors participating in a CBDC system. ([[.raw/papers/project-polaris-part-2-a-security-and-resilience-framework-for-cbdc-systems#Page 5|PDF p. 5]]).

## Method and evidence

- See Doerr et al (2022) for survey results on cyber threats and central banking. (PDF p. 5).
- This framework is CBDC-focused and leverages existing industry standards and guidelines, providing central banks with a seven-step model, as shown in Figure 1, for secure and resilient CBDC systems. (PDF p. 6).

## Limitations and cautions

- Many central banks already have robust cyber security and resilience measures in place and adhere to the highest of industry standards in controls and risk management. (PDF p. 6).
- However, risks cannot be fully eliminated and it is critical that senior leadership be aware of the potential new and elevated level of threats and risks facing CBDC systems so an appropriate risk management and mitigation strategy can be established. (PDF p. 6).
