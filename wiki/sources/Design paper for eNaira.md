Official central-bank technical report describing the design and rollout of Nigeria's eNaira, the second fully launched retail CBDC globally.

## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Documents eNaira's passive two-tier distribution model using Bitt Inc. as sole vendor, with full centralized transaction visibility by CBN. Smart-contract applications were technically functional but distribution incentives for commercial banks were lacking.
