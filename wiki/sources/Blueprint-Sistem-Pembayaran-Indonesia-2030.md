## Evidence-backed summary

- Continuity: the ID edition says the 2030 vision is "secara substantif tidak berbeda dari visi SPI 2025" ([[.raw/papers/blueprint-sistem-pembayaran-indonesia-2030-id#Page 9|PDF p. 9]]). Treat this as the BI-stated continuity, not as a claim-by-claim check.
- Digital Rupiah: the EN edition frames Digital Rupiah as expanding the scope of use cases and strengthening the first vision ([[.raw/papers/blueprint-sistem-pembayaran-indonesia-2030-en#Page 9|PDF p. 9]]). The ID edition describes it as oriented toward strengthening the same vision ([[.raw/papers/blueprint-sistem-pembayaran-indonesia-2030-id#Page 9|PDF p. 9]]).
- Policy objectives: the ID edition refers to three policy objectives on p. 9. The names are garbled in extraction and are not yet recorded.
- Infrastructure and connectivity: the figure list covers BI-FAST, QRIS, BI-RTGS, "Arah Pengembangan Rupiah Digital" (Gambar 14), and "Arah Strategis Konektivitas Lintas Negara" (Gambar 13) ([[.raw/papers/blueprint-sistem-pembayaran-indonesia-2030-id#Page 4|PDF p. 4]]).
