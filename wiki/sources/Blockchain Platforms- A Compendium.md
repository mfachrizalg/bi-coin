## Evidence-backed summary

- In Supply chain Management Blockchain enabled us to keep the track of the origin of the products. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-platforms-a-compendium#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- We can ensure the quality of the end products by maintaining the immutable record on the blockchain. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-platforms-a-compendium#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Further Blockchain is also used with IOT, medical services, baking, real Estate, etc. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-platforms-a-compendium#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- The main promising technology behind Bitcoin was ’Blockchain’. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-platforms-a-compendium#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Blockchain provided unique features like transactional privacy, system transparency, immutability of data, security with cryptography, etc. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-platforms-a-compendium#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- These features paved way for Blockchain in advancing many technologies like voting systems, IOT applications, supply chain management, banking, healthcare, insurance, etc. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-platforms-a-compendium#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Blockchain development was boosted with the increasing demand of the technological update. ([[.raw/papers/blockchain-platforms-a-compendium#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
