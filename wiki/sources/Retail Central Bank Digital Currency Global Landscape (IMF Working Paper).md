## Evidence-backed summary

- The Republic of Palau has partnered with Ripple to develop a U.S. dollar-backed digital currency for the country using the XRP Ledger. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-global-landscape-imf-working-paper#Page 3|PDF p. 3]]).
- If launched, it could be the world’s first government-based national stablecoin (GovCoin). (The Pacific island nation has no central bank and uses the U.S. dollar as its official currency, so a central bank digital currency (CBDC) is out of the question.) ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-global-landscape-imf-working-paper#Page 3|PDF p. 3]]).
- CBDC transactions will be subject to the existing riskbased AML/CFT PSP framework, including collecting full know-your-customer (KYC) information on all holders. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-global-landscape-imf-working-paper#Page 6|PDF p. 6]]).
- CB can see anonymized transaction data and outstanding CBDC in each digital wallet. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-global-landscape-imf-working-paper#Page 6|PDF p. 6]]).
- CBDC can be transferred in various offline media as a bearer instrument feature. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-global-landscape-imf-working-paper#Page 7|PDF p. 7]]).
- 2020. “Tiered CBDC and the Financial System,” European Central Bank WP No. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-global-landscape-imf-working-paper#Page 8|PDF p. 8]]).
- 2020. “Technology Approach for a CBDC,” Bank of Canada Staff Analytical Note 2020-6. ([[.raw/papers/retail-central-bank-digital-currency-global-landscape-imf-working-paper#Page 8|PDF p. 8]]).
