## Evidence-backed summary

- Besides countering private cryptocurrencies’ influence, central banks have also explored other benefits of CBDC, such as improving their financial oversight, enhancing digital payment systems, and reducing transaction fees and friction. ([[.raw/papers/assessing-disruptive-potential-of-retail-central-bank-digital-currency-and-influence-of-design-considerations-an-open-in#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- For several years, the Bank of Thailand has also invested in research and development of CBDC, including retail use cases. ([[.raw/papers/assessing-disruptive-potential-of-retail-central-bank-digital-currency-and-influence-of-design-considerations-an-open-in#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- However, an optimized design for an official launch of a retail CBDC in Thailand has yet to be determined, resembling fragmented progress observed among other central banks. ([[.raw/papers/assessing-disruptive-potential-of-retail-central-bank-digital-currency-and-influence-of-design-considerations-an-open-in#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This research has leveraged disruptive innovation theory to provide a fresh perspective on retail CBDC as potentially disruptive financial innovations. ([[.raw/papers/assessing-disruptive-potential-of-retail-central-bank-digital-currency-and-influence-of-design-considerations-an-open-in#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Additionally, it has proposed an open innovation-based methodol­ ogy to determine industry preferences concerning twelve design considerations for retail CBDC and their impact on nine indicators of disruptive innovation. ([[.raw/papers/assessing-disruptive-potential-of-retail-central-bank-digital-currency-and-influence-of-design-considerations-an-open-in#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Crosstab and logistic regression analyses have indicated that retail CBDC in Thailand could embark on a disruptive innovation tra­ jectory by adhering to defined design choices. ([[.raw/papers/assessing-disruptive-potential-of-retail-central-bank-digital-currency-and-influence-of-design-considerations-an-open-in#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Central banks worldwide are exploring Central Bank Digital Cur­ rencies (CBDC) for various reasons, e.g., to improve financial oversight, enhance security, and increase digital payment systems’ efficiency (Cirasino, 2021; Ozili, 2023; Schueffel, 2023; Xu, 2022). ([[.raw/papers/assessing-disruptive-potential-of-retail-central-bank-digital-currency-and-influence-of-design-considerations-an-open-in#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).

## Method and evidence

- Chapter three synthesizes twelve key design considerations and proposes an open innovation-based methodology to identify r-CBDC design preferences. (PDF p. 2).
- The subsequent chapter analyzes survey responses and their statistical interpretation. (PDF p. 2).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, an optimized design for an official launch of a retail CBDC in Thailand has yet to be determined, resembling fragmented progress observed among other central banks. (PDF p. 1).
- Journal of Open Innovation: Technology, Market, and Complexity 11 (2025) 100502 Critics of r-CBDC have also raised concerns about privacy, central banks’ reputational risk, decreased profitability and stability of com­ mercial banks, cybersecurity vulnerabilities, concentration of financial power, and infringement on people’s freedoms (Schueffel, 2023). (PDF p. 2).
