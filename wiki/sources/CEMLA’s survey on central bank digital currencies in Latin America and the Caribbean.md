## Evidence-backed summary

- The findings revealed prevalent issues and objectives regarding digital payments in the region, as well as promising avenues for central bank involvement. ([[.raw/papers/cemla-s-survey-on-central-bank-digital-currencies-in-latin-america-and-the-caribbean#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- However, it highlighted the challenges of creating a universal CBDC framework due to significant disparities among neighboring countries. ([[.raw/papers/cemla-s-survey-on-central-bank-digital-currencies-in-latin-america-and-the-caribbean#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- Moreover, this research effort sheds light on public perceptions, expectations, and concerns about CBDCs in Latin America. ([[.raw/papers/cemla-s-survey-on-central-bank-digital-currencies-in-latin-america-and-the-caribbean#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- By exploring various dimensions such as awareness, interest, adoption potential, concerns, preferences, and regulatory considerations, the survey offers a comprehensive understanding of the CBDC landscape in the region. ([[.raw/papers/cemla-s-survey-on-central-bank-digital-currencies-in-latin-america-and-the-caribbean#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This holistic approach provides valuable insights for policymakers, central banks, and stakeholders to navigate the complexities of CBDC implementation effectively and responsibly. ([[.raw/papers/cemla-s-survey-on-central-bank-digital-currencies-in-latin-america-and-the-caribbean#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- This survey seeks to critically examine the multifaceted implications of CBDC adoption in Latin America, addressing the promises and perils that lie ahead. ∗ Corresponding author. ([[.raw/papers/cemla-s-survey-on-central-bank-digital-currencies-in-latin-america-and-the-caribbean#Page 1|PDF p. 1]]).
- By delving into facets such as awareness levels, interest, adoption potential, concerns, preferences, and regulatory considerations, it paints a comprehensive picture of the CBDC landscape within the region. ([[.raw/papers/cemla-s-survey-on-central-bank-digital-currencies-in-latin-america-and-the-caribbean#Page 2|PDF p. 2]]).

## Method and evidence

- The survey extensively examined awareness and understanding of CBDCs across diverse stakeholders in Latin American nations, including the general public, corporations, and financial institutions. (PDF p. 1).
- By exploring various dimensions such as awareness, interest, adoption potential, concerns, preferences, and regulatory considerations, the survey offers a comprehensive understanding of the CBDC landscape in the region. (PDF p. 1).

## Limitations and cautions

- However, it highlighted the challenges of creating a universal CBDC framework due to significant disparities among neighboring countries. (PDF p. 1).
- However, limited engagement between central banks and legislative or executive branches suggests a need for more proactive collaboration in policymaking. (PDF p. 3).
