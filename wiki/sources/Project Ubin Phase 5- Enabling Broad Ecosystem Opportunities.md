## Supplied study type

Official central-bank technical report documenting Phase 5 of Singapore's multi-platform CBDC pilot, the most architecturally diverse PoC sequence among the nine cases.

## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Documents Singapore's progression from Ethereum-based central bank money tokenization (Phase 1, 2016), through multi-platform parallel testing (Phase 2, 2017), DvP and cross-border payments (Phases 3–4, 2018–2019), to government bond settlement on Quorum (Phase 5, 2020). Project Ubin produced Partior, the only transition from PoC to commercial infrastructure among the nine programs studied.
