## Method and evidence

- Specifically, we advance a theoretical model in which introducing an interest-bearing CBDC expands market participants' pay­ ment and investment options but also poses a potential threat to financial intermediaries if it raises their borrowing costs significantly. (PDF p. 1).
- This study contributes to the growing body of literature on CBDCs by advancing a theoretical model that examines the effects of the issuance of an interest-bearing CBDC on market participants' payment instrument choices and asset allocation decisions in the face of a network exter­ nality. (PDF p. 2).
