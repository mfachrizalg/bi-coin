## Supplied study type

Official central-bank working paper documenting the design, architecture, and rollout of China's e-CNY (digital yuan).

## Supplied abstract or takeaway

Describes a hybrid architecture combining centralized data management for real-time retail transactions with permissioned DLT for reconciliation, integrated with a four-tier KYC wallet system enabling tiered anonymity. Documents distribution through Alipay and WeChat Pay (cumulative 16.7 trillion yuan transactions) and dual offline capabilities via NFC cards, SIM cards, and wearables. Targets 300,000 polling/use stations.
