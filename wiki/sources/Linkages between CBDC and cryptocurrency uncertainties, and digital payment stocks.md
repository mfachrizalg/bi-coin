## Limitations and cautions

- Traditionally, the biggest players of payments markets were commercial banks and digital payment companies like VISA, Mastercard, and American Express, etc., However, the introduction of blockchain based cryptocurrencies has changed the sphere of payments market, especially after 2017 (Cross et al., 2021). (PDF p. 1).
- However, CBDCs solve the issue of legal tender (Bian et al., 2021), but these currencies will be centrally controlled by central banks and there are concerns about the data privacy in CBDCs (Jabbar et al., 2023). (PDF p. 2).
