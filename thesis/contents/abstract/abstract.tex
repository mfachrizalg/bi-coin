\textit{
This thesis implements and tests selected KYC and AML/CFT controls in a retail
Digital Rupiah prototype using Hyperledger Fabric 2.5. Its controls-study
extension uses a fresh four-organization network (CentralBankMSP, DistributorMSP,
ValidatorMSP, and EndUserMSP) and three Go chaincodes for monetary operations,
customer controls, and payments. Server-managed X.509 identities distinguish
the signing actor, customer subject, and wallet custodian.
}

\textit{
In run-20261014-01, executed under the earlier identifiers before the rename, the correctness check passed all 20 synthetic scenarios covering high-risk KYC
decisions with distinct reviewer and senior approver, restriction and
evidence-based release, reserve funding, retail payment, read-only ValidatorMSP
access, and expected endorsement failures. The re-run after the rename is pending, and unit-test results are not part of that record. These results establish behavior
only for the tested synthetic scenarios; they do not establish identity
authenticity, screening effectiveness, actual PPATK reporting, or institutional
compliance.
}

\textit{
As secondary performance evidence, Hyperledger Caliper measures the four-organization controls-study extension with one worker on one host. Those measurements do not establish production capacity or cross-machine reproducibility.
}
