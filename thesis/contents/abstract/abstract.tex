\textit{
This thesis aims to design and implement a financial compliance architecture for retail
Digital Rupiah on Hyperledger Fabric 2.5 that enforces the simplified customer due diligence (CDD) decision on the ledger, together with supporting AML/CFT controls, and to test that architecture functionally and to measure its performance with Hyperledger Caliper. A retail individual becomes an active subject only if a reviewer approves it, the match against the terrorist list (DTTOT) is negative, the subject is flagged low-risk, and its limits are set. Holdings follow two ceilings set by Bank Indonesia according to the verification status of the subject. The controls-study extension uses a fresh four-organization network (CentralBankMSP, DistributorMSP,
ValidatorMSP, and EndUserMSP) and three Go chaincodes for monetary operations,
customer controls, and payments. Server-managed X.509 identities distinguish
the signing actor, customer subject, and wallet custodian. A backend event consumer applies three monitoring rules to received transfers. Once an officer confirms an alert, it writes a draft suspicious transaction report (LTKM) and revokes the low-risk status without notifying the customer.
}
