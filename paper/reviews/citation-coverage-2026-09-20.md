# Citation coverage: paper and thesis

Date: 20 September 2026. Companion to [the joint review](joint-paper-thesis-review-2026-09-20.md) and [the remaining-work audit](remaining-review-audit-2026-09-20.md).

The compiled paper uses 19 distinct citation keys; the thesis uses 54. Their union contains **59 sources**, all inventoried below. Unused bibliography entries were excluded. Existing keys named `unknown...` are not, by themselves, evidence of unknown authors or fabricated references.

## Reading the coverage table

- **P**: relevant passages in an original paper/report or primary documentation were inspected. This does not mean every page of the source was read.
- **A**: verification is limited to an author/institution/publisher abstract, catalog, preview, or publisher-deposited abstract. Absence claims and detailed comparisons remain unverified unless separately noted.
- **M**: bibliographic identity was checked, but usable original text was not obtained. The manuscript claim is not cleared.
- **X**: the local source is a different publication from the cited record. It cannot validate the cited version.

“Supported” below refers to the stated use, not an endorsement of every sentence near the citation. Explicit exceptions and comparison defects are part of the result. Primary-source URLs and immutable local extractions are supplied so the author can reproduce each check.

## Explicit verification gaps

The source routes for `Pocher2022Privacy`, `unknown2022c`, `unknown2024f`, `unknown2025i`, `unknown2021`, and `logicmonitor2025pgmysql` did not yield usable original text. The actual 2025 `sethaput2025` publication is also still needed because the local PDF is a different article. Obtain these originals before calling their detailed claim support verified. Abstract/preview-only rows retain the stated limits.

Crossref checks were run for 33 active DOI records. Most resolved; arXiv's Crossref 404 was treated as a registration-agency limitation, and a rate-limited lookup was not treated as evidence of a nonexistent source. Chapter-title versus book-title fields were checked literally before reporting a mismatch. No citation keys, bibliography entries, raw sources, or manuscript wording were changed.
