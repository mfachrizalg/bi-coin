# CDD private data collection and off-chain placement

## Status

Accepted (2026-11-09). Its legal citations follow the dual mapping in ADR 0016, as amended on the same date. Amended (2026-11-25): record fields and off-chain placement change under ADR 0020 (see Amendment).

## Context

Before this decision, every peer stored every subject's CDD record in world state (`chaincode/controls/controls.go`, subject record and `subjectKey` at lines 41–58 and 516). The record included the restriction reason, so any organization could read it.

The backend wrote only `auth_users` to PostgreSQL. The thesis nonetheless said CDD identity data lived there (Batasan item 4), so the claim was false.

## Decision

1. The subject record lives in one private data collection, `cddCollection`, with members DistributorMSP, CentralBankMSP, and ValidatorMSP. It holds the whole record: status, limits, restriction, representatives, beneficial-owner references, document reference, and salt. The key stays `control~<subjectID>`. Staff records stay public.
2. Collection settings: `memberOnlyRead` and `memberOnlyWrite` are false, so access is enforced in chaincode. `requiredPeerCount` is 1, `maxPeerCount` is 2, and `blockToLive` is 0. The retention basis is UU No. 8 Tahun 2010 Pasal 21, which requires at least five years. This citation comes from the spec and was not re-verified in the legal check.
3. The subject ID is a readable argument: 16 random bytes, hex encoded, generated by the backend with `crypto/rand`. It is not the NIK. The NIK is stored only in `kyc_profiles.document_number`. The link between subject ID and NIK exists only in PostgreSQL.
4. All CDD contents arrive through the transient map, under the key `payload`, as JSON. Arguments carry only identifiers and payment amounts. The accepted leak is declared in the Batasan: non-members see which pseudonym had which action and when, but not the contents. Balances, receipts, and usage stay public.
5. The salt is 32 random bytes, hex encoded, generated per subject by the backend and sent in the transient payload. No pepper is used.
6. The identity document is stored off chain as `DOCUMENT_STORE_DIR/<sha256 hex>`. The default directory is `backend/data/documents`, with directory mode 0700 and file mode 0600. The `documentRef` is that SHA-256. Only PNG and JPEG are accepted, at most 2 MB. There is no download endpoint. Test data is a generated solid-colour PNG, never a real identity document.
7. Off-chain placement: `kyc_profiles` (legal name, document type, document number, document hashes) and `kyc_audit_events` are written in one SQL transaction before `CreatePending`. `retail_customers` and `kyc_provider_checks` are dropped. `payment_contacts` and `operation_journal` are unchanged. If the ledger call fails, the profile stays `pending`. PostgreSQL never authorizes a ledger effect (ADR 0007).
8. Purge is claimed, not implemented. Bab III names `PurgePrivateData`, and the implementation is listed as future work.
9. Regulatory basis. Bank Indonesia issues Rupiah Digital and regulates all PJSP. It sets the CDD rules for non-bank PJSP through PBI 10/2024, with the 2023 CDD Pedoman as guidance. OJK sets the CDD rules for banks through POJK 8/2023. Bank Indonesia's peer reads the collection to co-verify. Observers are OJK and PPATK, because a Distributor may be a bank or a non-bank. PBI 19/10/PBI/2017, cited in the earlier spec, was revoked by PBI 10/2024 with effect from 31 December 2024.

## Consequences

- `GetSubject` is allowed to member MSPs and to the caller who acts for the subject (`CanActFor`).
- The access rule for `CanPay` and `GetLimits` is member-or-actor. Transfer no longer calls them for the payee; `CheckPayee` runs on the endorsing members instead (ADR 0019).
- `GetSubjectHash` and the public commitment are removed (ADR 0019).
- Off-chain availability is not tested. Purge is not implemented.

## Amendment (2026-11-25): CDD Sederhana fields (ADR 0020)

- Decision 1 no longer lists representatives or beneficial-owner references. The record in `cddCollection` now holds status, limits, restriction, document reference, salt, the DTTOT result, the low-risk flag and its reason, the approval time, the verification state with its evidence reference and time, and the closure time and reason. Staff records stay public. A new public key `cdd_ceilings` holds the two Bank Indonesia holdings ceilings as integers and no personal data.
- Address, birth place, and birth date stay off chain. The transient payload of `CreatePending` still carries only the category, the salt, and the document reference. `kyc_profiles` gains `address`, `birth_place`, `birth_date`, `dttot_result`, `low_risk_reason`, `approved_at`, `verified_at`, `closed_at`, and `close_reason`, and its `status` follows the ledger status (`pending`, `rejected`, `active`, `closed`). The individual's identity fields are PBI 10/2024 Pasal 18(1)(a).
- New PostgreSQL tables: `monitoring_alerts`, `ltkm_drafts`, `customer_notices`, and a monitoring checkpoint table. LTKM drafts are not sent to PPATK. Confirming an alert changes the ledger only through `RevokeLowRisk`, so decision 7 holds: PostgreSQL never authorizes a ledger effect (ADR 0007).
- Decision 4 holds for the new transactions. `VerifySubject`, `UpdateSubject`, `CloseSubject`, and `RecordReview` send their contents in the transient `payload`. `RevokeLowRisk` and `SetCddCeilings` carry no personal data.
- Decision 8 stands. The 5-year purge is not implemented and is a Batasan.
