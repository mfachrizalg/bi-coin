# Thesis and prototype feature boundaries

KYC is implementation-only and excluded from thesis acceptance criteria. The canonical model is two-tier: Bank Indonesia issues w-Rupiah Digital directly into Distributor reserves held by Wholesale Banks and Retail Banks. Retail Banks convert those reserves into Retail floats of r-Rupiah Digital and act as Custodians for Retail Customers and Merchants. Observers cannot be Custodians.
